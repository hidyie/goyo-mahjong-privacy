Goyo Mahjong Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026
App: Goyo Mahjong (app.goyomahjong.game)
Developer: solmiir
Support and privacy contact: grnamu13@gmail.com

This policy describes the current Android testing version of Goyo Mahjong. The game is intended for people aged 13 and older. The initial test is planned for South Korea.

Game data on your device

Your puzzles, progress, collection, daily play records and preferences are stored on your device. No game account is required, and the game does not upload this saved progress to a game server. Core puzzles can be played offline. This version does not provide cloud saving or cloud restoration.

Advertising services

The testing version displays Google's official demonstration banner ads through Google Mobile Ads. It also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request advertising consent information. Test ads do not mean that these services process no data.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, app and advertising interactions, performance diagnostics, and device or other identifiers. Google describes these uses as advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. These SDK transmissions use TLS encryption. The game does not request GPS, contacts, camera or microphone access.

For details, see Google's advertising SDK data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. Third-party processing and retention are governed by the provider's practices; solmiir does not operate Google's advertising systems.

Privacy choices

The app checks UMP consent status before requesting ads. An advertising privacy-choice control appears in Settings when the SDK indicates that it is required. Available options depend on your region and the applicable advertising configuration. You can also use Android's advertising-ID controls. These choices do not necessarily stop all SDK processing. Offline play does not require loading an advertisement.

Support and requests

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as a problem description or screenshot. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot and handle your request. Support correspondence is handled through Gmail. Do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal information.

Contact grnamu13@gmail.com to ask about, access, correct or request deletion of information you have sent to us. We may need sufficient information to identify the correspondence. We cannot directly locate or erase records independently held by Google; requests about those records should use Google's privacy controls and request channels.

Retention and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until app storage is cleared or the app is removed. To delete it, open Android Settings, choose Apps, Goyo Mahjong, Storage, then Clear data. This erases your saved game and preferences, and cannot be reversed through a game account. The game does not create accounts, so there is no game account to delete.

Support messages are retained only as needed to handle the request, follow up on the issue or meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. You may request their deletion through the contact above. Clearing the app's local data does not delete email correspondence or data already processed by Google. See Google's data retention information for its services.

Sharing and security

We do not sell your support messages or local saved-game data. Service providers may process information as described above, and information may be disclosed when necessary to comply with a legal obligation. Services such as Google and GitHub may process data in countries other than your own. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

This policy's public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting that website may generate hosting logs, such as your IP address, under GitHub's Privacy Statement. The in-game policy copy is bundled with the app and can be read offline.

Young users

Goyo Mahjong is not directed to children under 13. Where local law requires a parent or guardian's permission, young users should obtain it before using online services or sending personal information to support. A parent or guardian who believes that a child has sent us personal information can contact us to request review and deletion.

Policy updates

We will update this policy when the game's data practices change. The effective date identifies this version. Privacy questions can be sent to grnamu13@gmail.com.
