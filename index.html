This policy describes the Android version of Goyo Mahjong. The game is intended for people aged 13 and older. No account is needed to play. A Korean version of this policy is included in the app.

Game data on your device

Your puzzles, progress, collection, daily play records and preferences are stored on your device. The game does not have accounts and does not upload this saved progress to a server operated by solmiir. Core puzzles can be played offline.

Backup and moving to a new phone

The app keeps one copy of your saved game in a backup folder on your device. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android includes that copy in the backup of your Google account, and it is also included when you copy your data to a new phone. This is how your progress returns after you reinstall the game or change phones. The backup contains game progress and preferences only. It is stored and transferred by Google and Android under your own account; solmiir cannot read it. You can turn Android backup off in your device settings; see Google's backup help.

No advertising

This version of the game shows no ads. It contains no advertising software and does not use the Android advertising ID. If a later version adds advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Analytics

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, so that difficult or confusing levels can be improved. It records gameplay events without your name or contact details: a level or daily board started, completed, restarted or stuck, with the level number, layout, time taken and the number of moves, hints, undos and shuffles; the tutorial completed; the album opened; the language changed; and a review request. Firebase also collects an app-instance identifier, device and app information such as model, operating system version, language and app version, and your country or region, which Google derives from your IP address. The advertising ID is not collected, and the data is not used for advertising or to build advertising profiles. Nothing you type is sent, because the game has no text entry. You can turn this off at any time: open Settings in the game and switch off "Send usage statistics to improve the game". While it is off the game sends no analytics events, and the choice is kept when you restart the game. These transmissions use TLS encryption. The game does not request GPS, contacts, camera or microphone access.

Google processes this data on our behalf. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's Privacy Policy.

Reviews and updates

The game may ask Google Play to show its review window or to offer an app update. Both are provided by Google Play; the game does not see what you write or whether you rated it.

Support and requests

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as a problem description or screenshot. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot and handle your request. Support correspondence is handled through Gmail. Do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal information.

Contact grnamu13@gmail.com to ask about, access, correct or request deletion of information you have sent to us, or to ask for the analytics data of your app installation to be deleted. We may need sufficient information to identify the correspondence or the app installation.

Retention and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until app storage is cleared or the app is removed. To delete it, open Android Settings, choose Apps, Goyo Mahjong, Storage, then Clear data. This erases your saved game, the backup copy on the device and your preferences, and it also resets the analytics app-instance identifier. A copy already stored in your Google account's Android backup is managed in your Google account's backup settings. The game does not create accounts, so there is no game account to delete.

Analytics data is kept by Google for the retention period set for the app's Firebase project, at most 14 months for event data. Support messages are retained only as needed to handle the request, follow up on the issue or meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. You may request their deletion through the contact above. Clearing the app's local data does not delete email correspondence or data already processed by Google. See Google's data retention information for its services.

Sharing and security

We do not sell your support messages, saved-game data or analytics data, and we do not share them for advertising. Service providers may process information as described above, and information may be disclosed when necessary to comply with a legal obligation. Services such as Google and GitHub may process data in countries other than your own. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

This policy's public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting that website may generate hosting logs, such as your IP address, under GitHub's Privacy Statement. The in-game policy copy is bundled with the app and can be read offline.

Young users

Goyo Mahjong is not directed to children under 13. Where local law requires a parent or guardian's permission, young users should obtain it before using online services or sending personal information to support. A parent or guardian who believes that a child has sent us personal information can contact us to request review and deletion.

Policy updates

We will update this policy when the game's data practices change. The effective date identifies this version. Privacy questions can be sent to grnamu13@gmail.com.
